## **FACEBOOK OUTREACH**

### Finding Prospects

**Option 1: Facebook Groups**

* Join business groups (search: entrepreneur, coaching, agency, marketing, consultant, CEO)

**Option 2: Friends Lists (BETTER)**

* Find an influencer/business owner in a group  
* Go to their profile → Friends list  
* Pick another influencer from their list  
* Keep jumping from friends list to friends list  
* This gives you unlimited, untapped leads

* Avoid anything that may make you look like a salesperson on your bio  
* Hide your location  
* Post Lifestyle,business/self-development and Growth content.  
* If you don’t have the bandwidth to post, REPOST influencers’ posts  
* COMMENT and engage your leads’ posts (helps them notice you faster)

### **Messaging Rules (CRITICAL)**

To avoid Facebook jail:

* Use 30 different versions of your opening message (1st message) daily  
* Our Managers will provide you the templates for opening message

# **LINKEDIN OUTREACH \- STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE**

A-List Influencers (50K+ followers)

* ❌ Don't target directly  
* ✅ Use them to find B and C-list prospects

B-List Influencers (10K \- 50K followers)

* ❌ Don't target directly  
* ✅ Use to find C-list prospects

## **STEP-BY-STEP OUTREACH PROCESS**

### STEP 1: Find A-List or B-List Influencers

Search LinkedIn for industry keywords:

* "Business coach"  
* "Marketing agency owner"  
* "Consultant"  
* "Online entrepreneur"  
* "Digital marketing expert"

Filter: Look for posts with high engagement (100+ likes/comments)

---

### STEP 2: Mine Their Engagement

**Method A:** Post Engagement Mining

1. Click on an A-list or B-list influencer's recent post  
2. Click on the "Likes" or "Comments" section  
3. Scan through the list of people who engaged  
4. Look for profiles with:  
   * Professional headshot  
   * Business-related title (Owner, CEO, Coach, Consultant)  
   * Under 7K followers  
   * Reasonably good profile

**Method B:** LinkedIn Events

1. Search for business-related events:  
   * Marketing summits  
   * Entrepreneurship webinars  
   * Industry conferences  
   * Networking events  
2. Click on event → "Attendees"  
3. Scan attendees for C-list profiles:  
   * Under 7K followers  
   * Business owner/coach/consultant titles  
   * Professional appearance

---

### STEP 3: Qualify Your Prospects

Before sending a connection request, check their profile for:

✅ GREEN FLAGS (Send Request)

* Professional headshot  
* Clear business description  
* Regular posting/commenting activity (at least in the last 45-60 days)  
* Less than 7K followers  
* Titles: Owner, Founder, CEO, Coach, Consultant, or something similar

❌ RED FLAGS (Skip)

* No profile photo or unprofessional photo  
* Empty bio or sparse profile  
* No recent activity, such as posts or comments (4+ months)  
* Corporate employee (not decision maker)

---

### STEP 4: Send Connection Requests

Daily Limits:

* If your account is aged and has been active  
  * Send 15-20 connection requests per day, maximum  
* If your account is new or has been inactive for a while  
  * Warm it up with 3-5 connection requests per day, maximum  
  * Increase this after the first week to 5-7 daily connection requests  
  * After the second week, you can start doing 10-20 daily connection 

* Spread them throughout the day (avoid bulk sending)  
* This will keep you away from LinkedIn restrictions  
  * Do not use a fake name on your LinkedIn

Pro Tip: Connection requests WITHOUT notes often get accepted faster on LinkedIn

---

### STEP 5: Message After Connection Accepted

**Timing: Wait 5-6 hours after they accept**

Before sending your first message:

* Go back to view their profile  
* Like and comment on their most recent (1-2) posts  
* Endorse (2-3) skills on their profile

**First Message Rules:**

* Make it conversational, not salesy  
* Reference something from their profile  
* Ask a question or give value  
* Here’s a sample below:  
  * *Hi Sha'ree, great to connect\! You have an amazing profile, and I really admire how you help high-achievers regain drive and balance through tailored coaching. We lead a community of founders focused on scaling with systems and organic marketing strategies. Would you be open to a quick chat this week to explore more about getting clients through Organic Marketing?*

---

### STEP 6: When They Reply

1. Don’t drag the chat too long  
2. If they say YES or sound positive, go straight to finding a good schedule for a call  
3. Any questions or objections they raise, you can always check objection handling sheet make sure your response will always redirect them back to jumping on a quick call  
4. If they say NO or sound negative, move on…except you wanna push a bit more

* You don’t have to post every day on LinkedIn or Facebook  
* Look for posts by influencers and repost them  
* Comment on your leads’ posts to get them to notice you

# **DM Setter Daily SOPs & KPIs**

## **Daily Prospecting Workflow**

**Platforms: Facebook & LinkedIn**

This workflow must be followed **daily** to keep accounts safe, activity natural, and results consistent.

---

## **1️⃣ Daily Warm-Up (Before Sending Requests)**

Before starting prospecting on **Facebook or LinkedIn**:

* Log in and spend **10–15 minutes** browsing your feed

* Like or react to **3–5 relevant posts**

* Leave **1–2 genuine comments** (not emojis only)

* Visit a few profiles naturally

✔️ This helps your account look human and active.

---

## **2️⃣ Sending Connection / Friend Requests**

### **Rules You MUST Follow:**

* Send **10–15 requests after 2 hours only**

* Maintain a **15–20 minute gap** between actions

* ❌ Do NOT send all 15 requests at once

* Keep activity slow and natural

### **Correct Example:**

* Send 3–4 requests

* Wait 15–20 minutes

* Send 3–4 more

* Repeat until you reach 10–15 in that hour

This applies to **both Facebook and LinkedIn**.

---

## **3️⃣ SOP Before Sending Any Request**

Before sending a request to **any prospect**, always:

1. Open their profile

2. Like **1–2 recent posts** (if available)

3. Check profile alignment with the niche

4. Then send the request (with or without a note as instructed)

❌ Never send blind requests without profile interaction.

---

## **4️⃣ Messaging Workflow (After Requests Are Sent)**

When sending messages:

* Follow the **same hourly rule** as requests

* Send messages in **small batches**

* Maintain **15–20 minute gaps**

* Never send bulk messages at once

### **Messaging Rules:**

* Use only **approved outreach scripts**

* Personalize lightly if required

* Keep tone friendly, professional, and human

---

## **6️⃣ Safety & Compliance Rules**

* Never rush actions

* Don’t message immediately after sending a request unless instructed

* Stop activity if you notice warnings or restrictions

* Always follow platform limits and SOPs

---

## **7️⃣ Quality Over Quantity**

* Focus on **right prospects**, not more prospects

* Clean prospecting leads to higher replies

* Your goal is **conversations**, not numbers

---

##  **Reminder**

This workflow is designed to:

* Protect your accounts

* Increase acceptance rates

* Improve reply rates

* Maintain professionalism with international clients
