# JentoAI Chrome Extension (Facebook & Instagram)

This extension acts as a bridge between Facebook/Instagram and your JentoAI CRM. It allows you to automatically extract leads from profile pages and send them to your CRM.

## How to Install (Developer Mode)

1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Go to the extensions page by typing `chrome://extensions/` in your address bar.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (the toggle switch in the top right corner).
4. Click the **Load unpacked** button.
5. Select this folder (`apps/chrome-extension`).
6. The extension will now appear in your browser. Pin it to your taskbar for easy access!

## How to Use

1. Click on the JentoAI extension icon in your browser toolbar.
2. Log in using your JentoAI agent email and password.
3. Select an active campaign from the dropdown.
4. Go to any Facebook or Instagram profile.
5. You will see a small JentoAI widget in the bottom right corner of the page.
6. Click **"Scrape & Save Lead"**. The profile details will instantly sync to your JentoAI CRM.
